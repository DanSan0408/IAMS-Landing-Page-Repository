The problem

Great assessment is hard to run.

Projects, presentations, portfolios, and peer review capture what exams cannot. But they are heavy to operate — and the logistics, not the pedagogy, are where courses get stuck.

Too many markers to coordinateSelf, peers, and one or more assessors, each on their own rubric and their own deadline.
Individual effort disappearsOne shared team mark hides who actually contributed.
Feedback arrives too lateComments reach students after the work is already done.
Evidence assembled by handOutcome attainment reconciled across spreadsheets, every term.
The platform

IAMS does the running. You keep the judgement.

Configure any assessment you can express as a rubric or checklist. IAMS collects the marks from every evaluator, reveals individual contribution, returns feedback, supports moderation, and exports auditable outcome evidence.

1

Configure

Set your assessment occasions, evaluator roles, and rubrics.

2

Evaluate

Each evaluator marks through their own rubric or checklist.

3

Compute

IAMS aggregates marks and applies the contribution factor — you moderate.

4

Deliver

Students receive feedback; you export outcome evidence.

What you get

Everything a multi-marker assessment needs.

One configurable engine, set up entirely by you — no fixed template, no discipline lock-in.

Configurable rubrics

Mix sub-rubrics, direct ratings, and comment questions on the same rubric, and reuse a saved blueprint to spin up a new one in a click.

As many markers as you need

Assign assessors by group, by rubric, or by individual student — self, peer, and group assessments are configured per student, per assessment.

Individual accountability

A self-and-peer contribution factor reveals who contributed to a team mark — computed transparently, and always yours to override.

Feedback that reaches students

Self, peer, and group comments are returned to students in one unified view, with peer identities kept anonymous.

Transparent from raw to final

Trace every mark through averaging and weighting, moderate any result, and export the full tabulation to Excel-ready CSV.

Progress tracking & reminders

Watch completion for every assessor and student on one dashboard, with automatic email alerts when an assessment opens or its deadline is approaching.
